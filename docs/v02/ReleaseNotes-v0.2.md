# Galactic Soup 0.2 - Combat Progression Alpha

## Start a new campaign

Galactic Soup 0.2 cannot continue a campaign saved by version 0.1.

When you open the game, an old campaign is described rather than offered for resume. It reads: "This saved campaign is from Galactic Soup 0.1." The game does not change or delete that save on its own.

To play 0.2, enter a pilot name and choose **Start campaign**. The game asks you to confirm first, because the new campaign replaces the old one and every save of it once it is saved. Choose **Keep the saved campaign** to back out.

Why: 0.2 reworks ships, fitting, weapons and encounters. A 0.1 campaign would not hold together under the new rules. Later releases are expected to carry 0.2 campaigns forward.

Campaigns saved by 0.2 itself resume exactly where you left them, including in the middle of a fight with missiles in flight or a web on your ship.

## What is new

### Three ships

- **New hulls.** Alongside the starter frigate, the station now sells a **destroyer** and a **cruiser**.
- **Bought bare.** A hull arrives with nothing fitted. Move weapons, ammunition and defences into it from the Fitting screen.
- **Your ships screen.** It lists every ship you own at the station and compares them. You switch between them instantly while docked.
- **Each ship keeps its own** cargo, fit, damage and insurance.
- **Trade-offs.** A bigger hull hits harder and lasts longer. It is also slower, easier to hit, and more expensive to fit, repair and lose.

### More ways to fit a ship

- **26 combat modules:**
  - projectile and hybrid turrets in small and medium sizes;
  - guided missile launchers;
  - active and passive shield and armour defences;
  - afterburners and capacitor support;
  - weapon upgrades;
  - a **stasis web** that slows an enemy.
- **16 kinds of ammunition.** Guided launchers can fire every damage type.
- **Guided missiles** fly to their target and hit on arrival. How much damage they do depends on the target's size and speed, not on turret tracking.
- **Market filters.** The market filters by purpose, by size, and by what fits your active ship.

### Twelve combat sites in three bands

- **Bands.** Entry, Developed and Advanced, all open from the start.
- **Before you warp,** each site shows its threats, recommended capabilities, rewards and possible loot.
- **Fit-sensitive sites.** Some sites punish a particular unsuitable fit, and say so in advance.
- **Five kinds of opponent:**
  - fast close-range frigates;
  - long-range artillery;
  - repairing brawlers;
  - missile boats;
  - web controllers.
- **Module drops.** Many modules now drop as loot. Everything you need can also be bought at the station.

## Known limitations

- **One star system and one station.** There is no skill system, and you cannot save fits.
- **Time.** Pause and 1x are the only time rates.
- **Saves and selling.** There are no manual save slots and no import or export of saves. Hulls cannot be resold.
